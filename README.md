# Undertone

Clothes, outfits and shopping matched to your colour type. A progressive web app (PWA): open it in a phone browser and add it to the home screen.

- Profile: colour type and palette, body shape, Kibbe or Larson style type
- Wardrobe: add your pieces (background removed on the phone), palette score, outfits around a piece, outfit diary
- Style: looks by occasion, 7-day planner with live weather (Open-Meteo, your location), trip planner and packing list
- Shop: catalogue in your palette with links to local stores; check an item from a photo
- Currency converter at the National Bank of Belarus rate

All data stays on the user's device (localStorage and IndexedDB). AI features are switched off in this version.
